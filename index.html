What your site is costing you — The Stirling Club

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a prospective member or a couple planning a wedding would. The writing is strong and Google can read it well. The leaks are in the last few steps.

1. "Join" doesn't take people to a form. On the membership page, the Join buttons scroll down to a section that only links out to a separate page on a different address. That's an extra click and a different-looking page at the exact moment someone wants in. Some people stop there.

2. Some of your best pages only say "call us." The micro-wedding, elopement, social club and amenities pages have no tour or inquiry button. Lots of people would rather fill in a form than make a phone call, especially at night.

3. Your homepage points people away from you. The first big button sends people to another company's ticket site for a ride. It sits right next to "Book a Private Tour" and competes with it.

4. On a phone the homepage stutters. In my phone test, the page was slow to respond and things jumped around while it loaded. A few behind-the-scenes files are also missing and come back as errors.

5. Shared links look bare. When someone texts a friend a link to your site, no preview image shows up.

What's working: your phone number is tappable on every page, the content is detailed, and the event pages answer common questions.

Unmeasured: visitor numbers, rankings, and whether inquiry forms reach your team. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.
