01

“Join” doesn't take people to a form.

On the membership page, the Join buttons scroll down to a section that only links out to a separate page on a different address. That's an extra click and a different-looking page at the exact moment someone wants in. Some people stop there.

The micro-wedding, elopement, social club and amenities pages have no tour or inquiry button. Lots of people would rather fill in a form than make a phone call, especially at night.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks the WordPress admin area. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Structured data describes the business

    The homepage includes business markup with your name, phone (702) 732-9700, address (2827 Paradise Road, Las Vegas, NV 89109) and opening hours. The phone number matches the page text and the call link.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML holds about 600 words of text and one main heading ("Where Las Vegas Exhales").

  • Gap
    No Google listing link on the homepage

    I found no link or embedded map pointing to your Google Maps listing, and the structured data has no links to your social profiles or other listings.

  • Note
    Business type in the data is a health club

    The markup uses the type "HealthAndBeautyBusiness". There's no event-venue type, so weddings and private events aren't described in that data.

  • Note
    Event pages live on more than one address

    Besides thestirlingclub.com, one of my search runs returned an event page at vip.stirlingclubnv.com, and a venues page appeared under www.thestirlingclub.com/public/. Search tools can see more than one place describing your events.

  • Note
    Your site's firewall blocked my first request

    When I fetched your site with a plain automated request from my server, it answered 403 Forbidden, including for robots.txt. A normal browser got through. I can't tell from outside whether real search crawlers are blocked.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns "not found". It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 15, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't have access to ask them, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.

What I'd fix first

Link your Google listing from the homepage and list it in your structured data, add an event-venue description alongside the club markup, keep event booking pages under one address, and ask your host whether the firewall lets real search crawlers through. I can't promise where it will place you.
